02 Musical Workframes

Four identities.
One sound studio.

Each project is its own sonic world. Press play to listen; only one track plays at a time.

Melodic House · Progressive

Syntric

An electronic music project exploring melodic house, progressive soundscapes, and atmospheric textures. A journey through rhythm, emotion, and sound.

Metalstep × Hybrid Electronic Metal

X-61

A hybrid electronic metal project fusing heavy metal riffs with half-time dubstep grooves, deep 808 bass, atmospheric pads, plucked synths, arpeggiators, and spectral soundscapes.

Heavy riffs. Electronic groove. Cinematic atmosphere.

Blues × Jazz Harmony × Funk Groove

Daisy Blue

A blues and jazz-funk oriented musical project blending soulful grooves, expressive distorted guitar solos, clean guitar fills, sophisticated jazz harmonies, and subtle sample-based textures. Rooted in blues expression and funk rhythms, Daisy Blue explores warm instrumental arrangements, melodic improvisation, and groove-driven soundscapes.

No fixed genre · Open experimentation

Ziad Mahjoub

An open creative space for musical experimentation, spontaneous sketches, and genre-blending. From cinematic soundscapes and expressive guitar to electronic explorations, orchestral textures, and unconventional hybrids, this project embraces freedom of expression without boundaries.

Some ideas are finished compositions. Others are simply sketches of something yet to come.
