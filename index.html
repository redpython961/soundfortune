Custom Composition

Original music, written for your project.

Custom composition is original music written and produced specifically for a client's project, rather than a pre-existing track or a generic instrumental. It can include original melodies, harmonies, chord progressions, arrangements, sound design, and production tailored to a brief, scene, brand, or artistic vision.

See starting rates
  • Film scoringOriginal music for a film scene, short film, or cinematic sequence.
  • Corporate musicMusic for a brand, presentation, or company identity.
  • Game musicThemes, ambient beds, cues, and soundtrack pieces.
  • Advertising musicA custom musical piece for a commercial or campaign.
  • Artist compositionOriginal melodies, harmonies, chord progressions, and arrangements for an artist.

How it differs from other services

Beat production
Creating an instrumental track, often for an artist.
Custom composition
Writing original music to meet a specific brief or creative purpose.
Mixing
Balancing and processing recorded or produced elements.
Mastering
Finalizing a mix for release and delivery.
