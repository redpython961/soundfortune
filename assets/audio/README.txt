Audio for the four music projects (Syntric – Purple Nights, X-61 – Parallel Voices,
Daisy Blue – The Blue Note, Ziad Mahjoub – Lotus) is published on SoundCloud and was
embedded on the original site through the SoundCloud player. SoundCloud does not offer
these tracks as downloads, so they are streamed through the official SoundCloud Widget
API, which drives the site's custom player.

To self-host instead: drop your own MP3 masters here (e.g. syntric-purple-nights.mp3)
and see "Switching to local audio files" in the main README.
